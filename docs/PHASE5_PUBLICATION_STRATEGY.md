## Positioning

Lead with an empirical study of **query information, experimental structure and relevance definitions in language-to-morphology retrieval**. The contribution would be a reproducible sensitivity analysis with biological interpretation and explicit failure conditions, not a model-count leaderboard. The completed audit supplies the cohort and information analysis; a manuscript still needs approved endpoints, scored experiments, uncertainty and validation. The 13-reagent strict non-control subset is too narrow for broad biological claims.

First approve the replicate unit, query-information condition, target mapping and evaluation population. Then run one narrow, paired experiment with controls and uncertainty. Add independent biological/visual evidence or a suitable independent cohort before broadening claims. Package executable audit code, safe aggregate tables, exclusion counts, provenance and limitations. Select Bioinformatics Advances versus BMC based on whether the final contribution is a generalizable evaluation insight or a rigorous reusable analysis with more limited scope. Reserve PLOS for evidence that supports broader methodological utility.

Do not spend time chasing additional workshops while the journal experiment remains undefined. Confirm authorship/contributions, ownership/license (existing #32), related-work disclosure, data-sharing approvals and publication costs with the team before submission.
